## Core Defense Statement

GroomShield combines context-conditioned DistilBERT scoring with LSTM-based behavioral sequence modeling to flag conversations for human review. F2-selected conversation cutoffs prioritize detection coverage while retaining precision. Applied to preserved scores for the author-disjoint test partition, the primary seven-feature LSTM detected 41 of 44 positives, achieving 0.9318 recall, 0.6613 precision, 0.8613 F2, and 0.9153 PR-AUC, with 21 false positives. Its F2 and PR-AUC advantages over the weighted scorer using the same seven inputs are supported by paired component-bootstrap intervals. The enhanced model detected all 44 positives with 41 false positives, demonstrating additional coverage at a greater false-alert cost. Section 3.5.1 gives the validation-only threshold procedure; Section 3.5.2 identifies the revised-policy analysis of previously evaluated test scores. Short-history detection, external contemporary-data transfer, and deployment performance remain the next evaluation stages.
